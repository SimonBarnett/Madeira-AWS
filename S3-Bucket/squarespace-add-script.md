# How to Add a Script Tag to a Squarespace Website Page

This guide explains how to copy and paste a &lt;script&gt; tag into a specific page on your Squarespace website. Follow these steps carefully to ensure the script works correctly.

## Prerequisites
- You have a &lt;script&gt; tag copied from our site.
- You have access to your Squarespace account (Business or Commerce plan required for JavaScript).

## Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Log in to Squarespace**  
   Open your browser, go to [squarespace.com](https://www.squarespace.com), and sign in to your account.

2. **Open the Site Editor**  
   From your Squarespace dashboard, select your website and click **Edit** to open the editor.

3. **Navigate to the Page**  
   In the Pages menu (left sidebar), click the specific page where you want to add the script.

4. **Access Page Settings**  
   Hover over the page title, click the **Settings** (gear) icon next to the page name.

5. **Open Advanced Settings**  
   In the page settings, click the **Advanced** tab.

6. **Add the Script**  
   - In the **Page Header Code Injection** field, paste the copied &lt;script&gt; tag.  
   - This adds the script to the <head> of the selected page only.  
   - If our site specifies the script should go in the footer, contact support for alternative methods.

7. **Save Changes**  
   Click **Save** to confirm your changes.

8. **Publish Your Site**  
   In the editor, click **Save** or **Publish** to make the changes live. The script will now run on the selected page.

## Notes
- Ensure the &lt;script&gt; tag is pasted exactly as provided, without modifications.  
- JavaScript requires a Business or Commerce plan; Personal plans don’t support &lt;script&gt; tags in Code Blocks or Injection.  
- If the script doesn’t work, confirm your plan and placement. Check in an incognito browser.  
- For issues, contact our support team or Squarespace Help Center.
